## Moving-star checks outside the control set

### ZTF18abgobyb

Gaia DR3 4293315765165489536 moves 1491.5 mas/year. The alert position is 0.131″ from the track segment between J2016.0 and the alert epoch. [Fink measurements](https://ztf.fink-portal.org/ZTF18abgobyb)

70 of 70 usable residuals are negative, spanning 47 UTC dates. Their median closest epoch along the Gaia track is 2018.35. The median distance from the star's position predicted at the time of each exposure is 9.29″. An old reference-image position could produce this pattern. The original coadd dates and image alignment provide independent tests of this interpretation. An independent check identified the star as VB 10. The anchor predicts a crossing around 2018-05-13, inside the 18-frame reference interval of 2018-03-21 through 2018-06-24. The April, July and October 2024 difference stamps each show a pair of opposite-sign residuals with similar orientation. Together the timing and morphology support foreground-star subtraction.
