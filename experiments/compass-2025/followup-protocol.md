# Direct-image checks on the three reserved cases

Frozen before reading the selected IDs or retrieving their direct light curves. Once the controls permit review, check the pattern against direct measurements from IRSA's ZTF DR24. Also inspect earlier catalog identities, alternative neighbors and multi-epoch images. Catalog recognition resolves priority for the source; the particular behavior requires its own measurement.

For each case, query DR24 series within 1.5″ of the anchor in g and r. Per filter, choose the series with the highest catalog observation count, then the smallest separation, then numeric ID. Retrieve that entire series. Keep zero catalog flags, finite magnitudes and errors in (0, 0.2] mag. On a UTC date use the median. Retain distinct exposure IDs. The primary comparison uses only dates through the anchor. Record archive cutoff and the later observations separately. Missing matches or inadequate overlap yield an inconclusive check.

* Recent onset. Compare the median on Fink positive dates to the median from 365 through 15 days before the first quality positive detection, in each filter. Require five baseline dates and three paired event dates to quote a difference. The static-source photometry may lack measurements during quiescence. Report any published name, discovery date or spectrum secured independently.
* Repeated bright detections. Use the first and last qualifying pairs supplied by the routing rule in its selected band. In each pair's inclusive date interval report direct-image coverage, median brightness, and its difference from the median on dates outside the two intervals. Require two in-interval dates per group and ten comparison dates. Check the other filter in the same intervals as a secondary measurement. A recurrent alert does not imply that an outburst occurred between the endpoints or that the star was quiescent between groups.
* Repeated fading. Compare direct median brightness on dates with only positive residuals to dates with only negative residuals in the same band. Ignore mixed-sign dates. Report the negative-date median minus the positive-date median if both groups have at least five measured dates. Check both bands. Retain the off-alert dates and their range to see how alert selection changes what is visible.

These are observational comparisons. Repeated validation on the same exposures cannot rule out shared calibration or blending. An independent catalog, direct cutoff or follow-up that disagrees with a route belongs in its case report.
