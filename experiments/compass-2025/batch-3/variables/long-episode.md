# Did MGAB-V243 stay bright in late 2020?

The registered red-group search found 19 bright dates between November 10, 2020 and January 17, 2021, a 68-day group. Earlier intervals between well-sampled bright groups were typically 14–20 days. A red measurement on November 5 preceded the group in the faint state, and another on February 7 followed it. A long run without a measured faint night can arise from cadence. These dates were selected in the primary red series.

Within the closed 68-day interval, count the primary red and green nights brighter or fainter than each band's frozen training quartiles. Report the observed range and largest gap. Recover all other same-position DR24 series in the original 1.5″ cone. For each detector series, derive the same quartiles from its own dates through the original training cutoff (at least 30 training dates); report excursions to its own faint quartile and which reached dates absent from the original red series. Record calendar dates and source IDs. In addition query the independent AAVSO observer archive from 30 days before the group to 30 days afterward and retain observer/band-matched, validated positive detections, separately identifying upper limits and ZTF-derived records. An independent faint detection during the 68-day group can split it. An empty or shallow archive supplies no evidence of persistence. The measured state differences in this dwarf nova alone cannot establish a Z Cam standstill or any particular accretion-disk mechanism.

**Independent survey photometry.** Query the ASAS-SN Sky Patrol master catalog within 6″ of the source. Use the nearest match within 3″, and report other returned entries. Where forced g flux is available, keep finite flux (including negative flux), positive finite error and good-quality measurements; use the nightly median flux. Compare the ordered ASAS-SN nightly flux to simultaneous ZTF green brightness, separately during the 68-day interval and on all shared dates. Establish the typical ASAS-SN flux on coincident ZTF bright- and faint-quartile dates outside the interval, then check how many independent dates inside had flux at or below the faint-state median. Preserve sampling, missing observations and the effects of blending. [Sky Patrol input catalog and photometry](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.03791)
