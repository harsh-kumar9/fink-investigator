# Pan-STARRS before the 2025 alert

Before retrieval, query the DR2 mean and stack catalogs within 10″ of the Fink anchor. Inspect every object within 1.5″, preserving aliases and catalog nondetections. For all such objects retrieve single-epoch detections. In the same filter, compare separate UTC dates whose measurements are within 1.2″ of the anchor, with positive finite flux and error, S/N ≥5 and PSF perfect-pixel fraction ≥0.85. Report a candidate change if at least three nights were measured in that band, the two most widely separated magnitudes span ≥1 mag and the gap exceeds five times their combined formal errors. Use one median per UTC date and the median single-exposure error per night. Without sufficient detections, keep a single-epoch peak as a lead requiring an independent baseline. Where a stack object exists, also seek forced warp measurements at its position, using returned errors and the 5σ limiting flux of usable observations to constrain individual faint epochs. A missing catalog row, an omitted exposure and a measured low flux are different evidence.
