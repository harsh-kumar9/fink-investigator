# Measure ZTF17aabutiy on the field's calendar

The cataloged target was missing on 13/55 early red dates, 4/36 later red dates, 15/50 early green dates and 9/33 later green dates. The strongest half-year difference used medians of 30-day bins. Bright- and faint-side nightly quantiles were much more similar across windows. This evidence motivated a forced, fixed-position pixel test registered before downloading science images.

Use every field-selected date from the recorded calendar in both windows and both bands. Keep its already chosen exposure, selected by the largest number of eligible controls and then its earliest time, independent of whether the target was cataloged. Retrieve a 120″ ZTF direct science-image cutout at the anchor sky position. Use the image WCS and a circular aperture of radius 2.2 pixels at the same celestial position at every epoch, even if the measured flux is negative. Subtract the median local sky in an annulus from 5 to 8 pixels, after rejecting points more than three robust standard deviations from its median. Repeat at radii 1.6 and 3.2 pixels. For local calibration select up to five field stars 10–45″ from the target with 14.5 ≤ mean magnitude ≤18.0 on the same detector and filter, at least 40 catalog measurements and no other such catalog source of any brightness within 5″ on that detector. Use the brightest first. Convert each aperture to mJy by the median ratio of catalog mean reference flux to measured positive reference counts on that image. Require two available reference stars and preserve every failure. Do not impute flux from the original target catalog.

Report the forced-flux quantiles and medians of the prespecified 30-day bins including visits missed in the published object series. Compare with catalog flux on matching dates, the raw observed bright-visit fraction at the fixed external-catalog threshold, the leave-one-bin-out changes, the sensitivity to aperture radius, and the behavior of the individual comparison stars calibrated by the other references. If the method cannot recover known positive target measurements, or fewer than half of field exposures can be measured, mark the result inconclusive. Nearby unresolved light can change the absolute zero and seeing dependence. The source image URL and SHA-256, WCS position and calibrated aperture pixel tiles are sufficient to replay extraction and audit individual dates.
