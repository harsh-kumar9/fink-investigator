# Pre-event optical counterparts to ZTF25aanlbls

Before consulting the Pan-STARRS photometry, query the primary DR2 stack and mean entries within 60″ of the alert. Compare all primary stack objects within 10″ with the median nightly Fink residual position, keeping individual multi-alert nights from dominating the center. Inspect deep g/r/i stacks and compare the local catalog PSF-minus-Kron magnitudes with matched nearby sources. A resolved galaxy has a positive PSF-minus-Kron difference and an extended profile relative to nearby known stars. Test the nearest plausible host against up to ten Gaia stars at 8–60″ with usable PS1 PSF and Kron photometry (15–21 mag in the chosen filter), prioritizing similarity in PSF magnitude then distance. Corroboration in two filters and visual inspection would strengthen a host interpretation. A faint or absent Gaia counterpart does not establish galaxy membership. Retain alternate neighbors, nuclear transients and foreground stars. Report whether astrometric scatter is large enough to encompass the apparent displacement.

After seeing the DR10 catalog classification (round exponential) and before measuring the DR10 pixels, test the model on the public grz image stack. Use the nearest Gaia star as the empirical point-spread function. In each filter, scale it to the target's measured 1.8″ aperture flux and inject into fixed grid positions at least 6″ from every DR10 source. Compare the ratio of flux within 0.8″ to that within 1.8″ at the source and at the injections. Keep the target and injection pixel phases equal. This tests whether the local seeing and noise alone can explain the profile. Neither the filters nor the original and repeated Tractor releases are independent trials.
