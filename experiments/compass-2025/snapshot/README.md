# Calendar-year archive and missing-history check

The twelve monthly Fink anomaly requests returned 2,170 rows. The service included 60 boundary packets in two adjacent requests. Applying each half-open UTC month once leaves 2,110 distinct alert packets on 211 nights, from 974 ZTF identifiers. November returned an empty list. The archive alone does not establish whether ZTF observed during that gap. [Sampling protocol](../protocol.md) · [Fink anomaly selection](https://doc.ztf.fink-broker.org/services/search/anomaly_detection/)

The registered sampling rule selected 132 anchors. Their strata contained 17 with reported first detections at most 90 days earlier, 55 other positive alerts and 60 remaining alerts. Thirty-three four-ID requests retrieved their object histories. For 131 anchors, their candidate identifier and every requested ZTF measurement agree between the anomaly and history endpoints. These sources contributed 32,063 distinct detections through their respective anchor times.

**Missing-history amendment.** During capture, before the candidates were ranked, we found one selected anomaly packet absent from its object's returned history. Repeat the public history request by ID for each such case, preserve both responses, and abstain for the original slot if the anchor remains absent. ZTF18abncmxb's 2025 candid 3105393542015015010 is present in the July anomaly response. Both the batched history and the individual retry returned just three 2026 detections. The replay returns an empty usable history for this ID, retaining its anchor and recording the abstention in the coverage output. [Independent single-ID response](retry-ZTF18abncmxb.json.gz) · [Original URLs, parameters, retrieval times, SHA-256 and sample IDs](manifest.json)

From the repository root, `python3 compass_archive.py replay --snapshot experiments/compass-2025/snapshot` verifies every receipt and checksum, recomputes the sample, and outputs the complete coverage accounting. To capture another year, pass an unused destination to `python3 compass_archive.py fetch --year 2024 --snapshot experiments/compass-2024/snapshot`. This selects up to four anchors per stratum per month and fills unused slots from the remaining anchors. Pass `--per-stratum 1` to limit a run to three histories per month. Repeating an interrupted fetch resumes its partial snapshot.
