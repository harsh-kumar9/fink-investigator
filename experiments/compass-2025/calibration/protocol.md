# Real-source controls for the three search routes

The expectations and UTC bounds were fixed before retrieving the extra first-detection fields or scoring these cases. For each ID choose the latest g or r alert in its control interval with DRB ≥0.8 and a magnitude uncertainty in (0, 0.2]. Resolve time ties by smaller candidate ID. Use its contemporaneous first-detection field and all available detections through that packet. Treat a missing anchor as an unevaluable control. Never substitute the first stored Fink row for the first detection reported by ZTF. Each route must recover all its registered positive and negative outcomes to pass its control set. Unspecified outcomes receive no label. Inspect the original seven-source moving-star screen separately. It tests a different failure mode.

The supernova control is a fresh published object. The stellar controls reuse known identifications and have historical anchors ending before 2025. These choices check whether the alert measurements reproduce the expected behavior under the stated cadence and quality cuts. They cannot establish population sensitivity, purity or the source type of a new candidate. Exclude the registered controls and their matching positions from all candidate slots.
