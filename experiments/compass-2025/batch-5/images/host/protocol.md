# Pre-event light at ZTF25absffwy

After identifying the southern arm visually on Subaru HSC and DECam images, measure its position relative to the spectroscopically observed nucleus. Keep the nearer Tractor entries as positional alternatives and check their locations against the images. Compare the g, r and z median intensity in a 1″ aperture at the transient to a fixed grid of sky apertures on the two flanks of the galaxy. Use locations at least 30″ in the east–west direction from the nucleus and at least 5″ from a catalog source. A control may contain faint, uncataloged or outer-disk light. Check for a local peak by comparing a central 0.5″ aperture with an annulus at 1–2″, and inspect the same contrast around the 12 equally spaced positions on a 3″ ring about the transient. These local measurements describe structured light and cannot establish a calibrated progenitor flux limit.

In the first high-pass check a weak local maximum appeared near the alert in Subaru grz and DECam gr. For an exploratory sensitivity check, repeat the small-minus-broad Gaussian blur with a fixed 0.22″ narrow sigma and broad sigmas of 0.9″, 1.5″ and 2.5″. Record the distance to the nearest positive local maximum in each image band, including DECam z. For a local comparison, at the 1.5″ scale require a positive peak within 0.5″ in all three Subaru bands and in DECam gr. Test a fixed 2″ grid within 20″ of the alert, omitting its central 3″, known Tractor centers within 1″, and positions with Subaru r broad-background light outside two thirds to 1.5 times that at the alert. These sky positions sample the real arm and retain uncataloged knots. The location and repeated band pattern suggested this test, so the result is descriptive.

The HST archive covers the field in F555W and F814W before the event. Inspect both images and the HAP source table. Register the HST source positions to independent Gaia DR3 stars with measured proper motion, RUWE ≤ 1.4 and unsaturated photometry (flags 0 or 1 in both HST bands). Match within 0.8″ after propagating to the exposure date, using an unweighted median translation. Report individual Gaia residuals and scatter. Record the nearest HAP sources passing flags 0 or 1 separately for each filter. Measure a fixed 0.15″ radius aperture at the registered event position with median local sky from 0.25–0.45″. Compare it to identical apertures near the same arm that are separated from accepted HAP sources and have broadly similar background. These describe local recovery conditions. A physical progenitor would also require differential astrometric registration to the transient itself and independent evidence of its disappearance.
