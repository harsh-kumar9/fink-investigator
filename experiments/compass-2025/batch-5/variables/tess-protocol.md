# Contemporaneous TESS evidence for dwarf-nova bright groups

Recorded before inspecting TESS sector metadata or pixels. Seek independently measured episodes and, for MGAB-V306 (a suspected SU UMa star), superhump-like oscillations during a long bright group. Query the public TESSCut sector inventory for the two positions. Request the sectors whose observing dates overlap the four primary ZTF long groups of MGAB-V306 and the five clustered Aug–Oct 2019 short groups of MGAB-V960. If none overlap for a source, measure all sectors through September 2025 for MGAB-V306 and at most the earliest four for MGAB-V960. Preserve sector choices, observation spans, fill gaps, target pixels, comparison apertures and source provenance. Never use a nonoverlapping sector to verify the 2019 timing. For diffuse or blended scenes require time-correlation with ZTF direct points and a localized image difference, allowing one TESS pixel of error (TESS pixels are about 21″).

For MGAB-V306, take only time-covered ZTF groups or independently recovered flux plateaus with ≥5 d of consecutive usable high-state coverage. Search periods 65–150 minutes in first half of each accepted plateau after subtracting a one-day trend and normalizing locally. Hold the phase fixed on the second half. Record the number of frequency, sector and window trials; compare predictions with adjacent pixels and other TESS apertures, gaps and the cadence Nyquist limit. Require a positive time-transfer, localization of modulated light and repeatability over more than one group before interpreting a periodic signal or assigning a subtype. If no suitable plateau was observed, the subclass test is uninformative.

For a plateau selected from TESS alone, use the target's corrected central 3×3 light curve on six-hour medians. Register a bright bin above its sector median plus 1.5 times the robust scatter (1.4826 × median absolute deviation). Require ≥5 days of bright bins separated by at most 0.5 days and ≥70% fill in that span. Use only a sector where matched ZTF red or green achieves rank correlation ≥0.65 on ≥6 dates and the brightest corrected difference pixel is within one pixel of the target. The period search eligibility gate prevents a blended star's modulation being attributed to this object. For all 2019 MGAB-V960 peak comparisons, subtract medians of the six-hour images within ±1.5 d of nearby bracketing faint dates from the median within ±1.5 d of the observed ZTF red maximum, omitting overlapping bins. Use faint dates within 10 d of the peak and record when only one side was covered.
