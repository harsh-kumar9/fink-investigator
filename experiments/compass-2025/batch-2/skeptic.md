# Independent first reading

These assessments use only the three case records in the batch-2 packet.

* **ZTF25aacfjde.** Four positive dates in two colors support a short brightening. The reported 13-day age dates the alert activity. A positional coincidence with an existing variable or an outburst of the candidate white dwarf would also explain it. The empty TNS label leaves priority open. Check the offset from the propagated star and the extent of the reference light, and search for pre-event measurements and later returns to baseline.
* **ZTF18aagstwh.** Both signs occur on many dates across several years. A known pulsator sampled at different phases can meet this fading rule. A close neighbor, motion or reference subtraction can shift the inferred source. Compare the signs against direct magnitudes, test a catalog period prospectively, and compare residual positions to Gaia including motion.
* **ZTF18abmfrjj.** The two green pairs are separated by six years and have no measured baseline in the packet. Continuous variability or a reference offset can supply both pairs. The catalog lookup failed. A 13.55-mag residual merits checking saturation or neighboring bright light in science and reference frames. Check the difference-image position on several dates, direct-image brightness in both windows, and nearby stars' changes.
