# Direct-photometry cross-check

Registered after identifying the VSX-empty cases and before retrieving direct-image light curves. Six of the seven objects recur over multiple years. A star in a position catalog alone does not establish which source varied. Test whether the original images independently reduced by the ZTF direct-photometry pipeline show the expected sign of change at the alert epochs. The measurements share a telescope and exposures, so this check cannot rule out blending or shared calibration errors.

Check all six long-lived VSX-empty objects, including the one found in the service-failure audit. Calibrate the calculation first with ZTF18achfhai, a SIMBAD-identified RR Lyrae star in the frozen 20-object cohort. The reported supernova has its own transient registry and spectroscopic check.

* Query the original-image r-band light curves within 1.5″ of the anchor via IRSA. Take measurements through the anchor only, with catalog flags zero, reported uncertainty in (0, 0.2] magnitude, and at least 30 points in the source series. Keep direct-photometry object IDs separate. Compare to quality-selected r-band Fink detections within 60 seconds, one-to-one; use the direct series with the most matched dates, breaking ties by source ID.
* Convert each difference magnitude to signed relative flux and each direct magnitude to relative flux. Use one median per UTC date and require at least 20 matched dates. Report rank correlation and the difference in median direct magnitudes between positive- and negative-residual dates. For the latter require five dates in each group. The public archive may end before the alert.
* A correlation ≥0.7 and a brighter direct median on positive dates supports variability at the matched position. Require this outcome on the RR Lyrae control before interpreting the threshold for unclassified objects. Report all results including weak, one-sided or undersampled outcomes. These are operational checks of two reduction products, not calibrated false-discovery probabilities or classifications.
