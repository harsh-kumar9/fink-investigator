# Independent skeptic's first reading

At ZTF18abjtfpx the 5.81178-day red-band model improves prediction across 153 later nights. I would retain this as a timing lead. The largest remaining threat to identifying a new variable is source confusion. Two other Gaia detections lie within four arcseconds and a catalog compiled at lower resolution could report the same light through a different identifier. The intrinsic cycle could also be a harmonic or a cadence alias.

A prior variable-star entry with matching coordinates and timing would settle prior reporting. A matching period in a neighboring source resolved independently from the closest Gaia star would reverse the source attribution. A different cadence can distinguish a short true cycle from its daily aliases. Prior to any new lookup I assign no physical class or unique catalog identity to the clock.
