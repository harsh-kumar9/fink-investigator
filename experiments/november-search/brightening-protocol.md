# Does the brighter red state recur in the green band?

An exploratory check of all clean nights in the red series 563212100016993 for ZTF18abqclig showed lower magnitude medians in 2023–25 than in 2021–22. On November 25, 2026 we fixed the following cross-band test before retrieving the direct green-band light curves or the field comparison data. The red data selected the source and intervals.

* Use ZTF DR24 green-band direct photometry in the red series' survey field, CCD and quadrant. Select the nearest green catalog position within 1.5″ of the alert with at least 100 observations. Use up to five comparison stars 10″–120″ away on the same quadrant, with at least 100 observations and catalog mean brightness within one magnitude of the target's. Rank by smallest absolute mean-magnitude difference, then by separation and object ID. Skip comparison sources within 5″ of another selected comparison. Apply no variability filter to the comparison stars.
* For exposures from 2021-01-01 through 2025-12-31 UTC, require zero catalog flags and 0 < magnitude error ≤ 0.2 for all included measurements. At each target exposure require at least three of the fixed comparison stars. For each available comparison subtract its median over the full test interval, then subtract the median of those comparison residuals from the target magnitude. Collapse the corrected target measurements to one median per UTC date. Use all qualifying dates, including those with no Fink alert. Record data coverage and annual medians.
* The shift is the early median minus the late median. Require at least 20 usable target dates in each interval, a corrected shift of at least 0.5 mag toward brighter values, and a shift in the same direction against at least three individual controls on paired dates. Record raw comparison star shifts. Test the alternative of seasonal sampling by repeating the calculation using only calendar months represented in both intervals, giving each common month equal weight. Report any disagreements. These checks assess a change in the sampled magnitude distribution and leave the varying component and physical mechanism open.
