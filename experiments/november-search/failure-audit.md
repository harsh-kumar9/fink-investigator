The anomaly response showed a failed CDS lookup on every in-window packet. Before inspecting the new SIMBAD or VSX matches, we selected the same recovery used in October. Start with all failed crossmatches, inspect the independent 3″ SIMBAD cone, and check a 5″ VSX cone for each remaining position. Preserve the frozen primary disposition. Record ambiguities, exact catalog identifiers, physical aliases and whether the original photometric quality cuts pass. [October method](../october-census/protocol.md)

Review up to three previously unexamined positions in pilot score order that satisfy the original photometric cuts, lack a supplied TNS name and remain open after the catalog searches. For each, check the Gaia DR3 source and variability tables and both Chen et al. ZTF variability tables within 5″, inspect available images across three epochs, and apply the existing direct red-band photometry test. Apply unchanged cuts on time matching, matched dates, residual signs and brightness agreement. Attribute each lead to the failed-crossmatch audit. A source with too few direct observations for the test has an inconclusive direct result.
